## Artificial intelligence

The project is made by gReeD034 with the help of three AI tools:

- **Claude** (Anthropic, Opus models including Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5): game and tool code, analysis, debugging, documentation, under the direction of gReeD034, who reviews everything and tests on the console.
- **ChatGPT** (OpenAI): images generated by gReeD034 (ground textures, icons, portraits, illustrations).
- **Meshy**: 3D models generated by gReeD034 under a private license, then retouched and animated by script.
